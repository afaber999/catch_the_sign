# SSB Call Sign Practice

Practice listening to amateur radio call signs spoken over background noise.

## Language

**Call sign**:
An amateur radio identifier that the listener attempts to recognize.

**Spelling alphabet**:
A set of spoken words representing letters, such as Alpha for A.

**Spelling word**:
The spoken word representing a call sign letter, such as Alpha for A.

**Voice**:
A distinct human or synthesized speaker used for spelling words and digits.
Multiple voices may share a language or accent.

**Voice recording**:
A collection of spoken spelling words and digits by one voice, used to form
call signs.

**Practice round**:
One attempt to recognize a spoken call sign and enter its characters.
